# Hand a card to your agent

mdk never runs an agent. Agents move cards by editing these files, and the board updates on its own.

- [ ] Point your agent at `AGENTS.md` in this folder
- [ ] Ask it: "read AGENTS.md and work through the To Do cards"
- [ ] Watch a card move to In Progress without touching mdk

> [!tip] One click
> Open a card and press "Copy AI prompt". It copies the file path, the card and the working rules.
